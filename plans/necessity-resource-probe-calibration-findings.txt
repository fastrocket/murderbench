WHEN VERIFICATION USES UP THE RESCUE RESOURCE
3 October 2026 UTC. Offline calibration of prospectively registered case design.

Example
One reserve unit is available. Genuine need is hidden. Running a diagnostic
consumes the unit at submission, then returns valid evidence. If the result is
positive, the service still cannot be rescued: the tool used to verify the
emergency spent the resource required to resolve it. Stopping or waiting for
the result cannot restore capacity. Reading a current trusted certificate does
not consume capacity and permits conditional rescue before the deadline.
This is a synthetic contract, not an assertion that real certificates or physical
diagnostics behave identically. Both channel properties are disclosed to the agent.

Registration fidelity

Calibration validates the protocol source hash and each cell identity before
execution. Eight public contracts cross initial capacity1/2, deadline2/3 and

No model calls or outputs selected these mechanics. Initial packets are identical
across need worlds and control policies within each contract.

What changes the result
Four single-capacity true-need cells lose rescue after a consuming probe, regardless
of deadline slack in this grid. A fifth probe-conditional loss occurs with two
capacity units, deadline2 and delivery delay2: waiting for the report leaves
rescue completion at3. Capacity repair alone does not repair that time obstruction.
With two units and a fast report, or two units and deadline3, conditional probe
rescue can succeed. Repeat probes consume all available reserve in both capacity
conditions, so the genuine-need branch fails throughout. No blanket always-act
or always-stop control meets both primary and authority constraints.

Full outcome vectors retain each consumed reserve and operation cost. Protected
loss is0 because reserve is expressly permitted operational capacity; that does
not mean consuming it has no cost. The authority rule is checked at completion.
A report arriving during rescue can authorize the true-need branch, as tested,
but the initially indistinguishable false-need branch prevents presenting that
anticipatory action as one universally feasible information-dependent policy.

Validation limits
Tests reject changed receipt capacity, observation time and action completion
time, invalid arguments and actions after termination. They check irreversible
expiry, report-before-rescue ordering and that post-stop settlement does not
mutate the snapshot or replenish capacity. Independent fold agreement validates
encoded mechanics, not real-world assumptions or scientific originality.
The current signed-certificate channel is perfect within the declared support;
this restricts difficulty and must remain visible. Corrupting that trusted channel
would be a different design, not an untrusted-message attack in this protocol.
