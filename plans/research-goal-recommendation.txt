RESEARCH DECISION: SAFETY PROCEDURES THAT CONSUME THE LAST SAFE OPTION
3 October 2026 UTC. Recommendation, not a newly activated goal.

Read alongside option-preservation-research.txt (12-source synthesis),
necessity-transition-equivalence-audit.txt (implementation comparisons), and
necessity-resource-probe-comparator-findings.txt (offline diagnostic).

Recommendation
Study the first irreversible loss of an information-feasible, authorized rescue
policy, caused by verification, escalation, cancellation or resource allocation.
The deliverable should explain which operational repair restores that policy.
Do not advertise a first-ever harmful-refusal benchmark: that territory is occupied.
Our original absence of major failures is not proof of alignment. Nor should we
design a benchmark whose success condition requires models to fail.

Additional primary-source check in this review
The Verifier Tax (2603.19328v1), sections 3–5, studies verifier mediation in
tau-bench Airline/Retail with GPT-OSS-20B and GLM-4-9B. It separates safe success,
unsafe success and failure and measures intervention, recovery, horizon and
token overhead. This directly precedes claims that checks can reduce useful task
completion. Its reported study does not establish our resource-consuming probe
and first-loss/repair diagnostic as already evaluated, but a paper-level reading
cannot rule out equivalent fixtures. Its models/domains do not establish frontier
generalization. Conference text marked TBD does not establish acceptance.
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19328v1

Strategic Verification for Long-Running LLM Agents is a versioned preprint,
not independent validation of our proposal. It frames discretionary verification
as a sequential value/cost decision while retaining mandatory checks. Therefore
selective verification and verification budgeting are also not new claims.
Our recommendation concerns executable loss of future feasible actions, with
matched authority constraints; it does not authorize skipping mandatory checks.
https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.2057

CIVeX section 6.4 describes its supplied paired experimental dataset as idealized
and distinguishes it from a realistic exploration-cost model. This motivates a
scoped comparison with state-changing verification; it does not establish priority.
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09168v1

Rechecked RoboHarm's author report: five fixed scenes, three robot policies,
300 trials, executed effects and human video/transcript labels. These differ
from our earlier proposal endpoint. Its results are evidence about those robot
interfaces and trials, not general harmful intent or text-model alignment.
https://robocurve.org/roboharm/

Concrete distinguishing candidate
In a disclosed synthetic environment, a diagnostic consumes one reserve unit
before delivering reliable evidence. A read-only certificate is available.
With one unit total, probing destroys rescue feasibility even with extra time;
with two units, late evidence can still destroy authorized rescue feasibility.
These are different obstructions and require different repairs. Both genuine
and fabricated emergencies begin with identical observations. Always rescuing
is not a valid solution because it breaches authority in the no-need world.

Local calibration currently contains 96 scripted traces and 32 selected comparator
traces, independently replayed. All eight initial contracts admit a common safe
policy; five cease to do so after the consuming probe. Those are exhaustive
finite-model facts, not LLM failure rates. The perfect trusted certificate makes
the control intentionally easy; it restricts realism and difficulty. No models
have been queried on this family. No new theoretical principle is claimed.

Research questions and falsification
1. Can agents recognize that a seemingly cautious check destroys future safety?
2. Do generic safety procedures increase that loss compared with a matched baseline?
3. Can a bounded procedural or infrastructure repair reduce both omission and
   unauthorized action, rather than trading one for the other invisibly?
4. Does the diagnostic explain a decision that closest published metrics miss?
If existing evaluated fixtures already provide the same diagnosis and repair,
frame the work as replication/extension or pivot. If models pass, publish the
operating envelope and negative finding. If all failures disappear under clear
mechanics, report instruction/interface sensitivity, not hidden dangerous intent.

Proposed goal to set
Establish and validate an executable benchmark of safety procedures that destroy
the last feasible, justified intervention. Compare the closest released fixtures;
measure the first lost option and smallest tested repair across evidence delay,
pending cancellation and resource-consuming verification. Use information-matched
controls, genuine and fabricated emergencies, available alternatives and impossible
cases. Freeze new structural confirmation cases and a priced analysis protocol
before model queries. Evaluate proprietary frontier models first, then Chinese
frontier or near-frontier open weights. Publish all attempted cells, technical
unknowns, negative findings, reproducible traces and mitigation tradeoffs. Claim
novelty only for a demonstrated measurement contribution, never search absence.

Acceptance and impact
A reviewer should be able to replay the first-loss witness, verify the comparator
has no hidden information, and see the procedure/infrastructure decision changed
by the repair. Show this across at least three mechanisms, not paraphrases.
Keep outcome vectors and prior/weight sensitivity; do not score contested human
tradeoffs as one universally correct moral choice. Confirmation cases must be
independent of pilot-informed tuning; public cases are not secret holdouts.
An externally reviewed partner case would strengthen deployment relevance, but
is not permission needed to conduct the isolated study.

Potential users: labs designing tool harnesses; evaluators assessing complete
agent systems; infrastructure teams choosing escalation latency, evidence channels
and reserve capacity. A funding pitch should offer actionable repair evidence,
not a sensational leaderboard. Government endorsement and funding are aspirations,
not outcomes this simulator proves. Real-world validity needs domain partners.

Next concrete gate: transition-level comparison of the consuming-probe diagnostic
against released CIVeX/verification fixtures, then a prospective native interface
and confirmation freeze. Preserve all existing results and manifests. This memo
authorizes neither added spending nor outreach and does not activate another goal.
