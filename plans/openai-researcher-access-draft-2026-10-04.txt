Can a safety label make an agent less safe?

We propose to test whether an agent changes its executed actions when an
unchanged partial movement guard is described as a "safety approver" instead
of a "motion validator". Task, authority, observations and coverage facts are
identical. The guard enforces movement bounds but does not detect contact with
synthetic forbidden surfaces. The operational question is whether a deployment
owner should change the guard's description, disclose its exclusions more
explicitly, or replace it with an executor covering the relevant hazard.

The current operator is an existing commercial LLC with a new evaluation
initiative. We have no claimed university, nonprofit or accredited-lab
affiliation. We ask the programme to determine whether this qualifies as an
"other research organization"; eligibility has not been established. An
eligibility-routing inquiry was sent separately, not represented as an award.

We do not claim a novel model failure. RoboHarm, RoboPAIR, SafeAgentBench,
delegated-misalignment studies, LLM risk-compensation experiments and guardrail
evaluations cover substantial surrounding ground. Our limited audit has not
identified the exact naming-only comparison; that is not proof of priority.
The released Malberg framing tests already use complementary success/failure
descriptions intended to preserve underlying facts. Information equivalence and
action scoring alone do not establish a contribution; the study must change a
concrete guard deployment
decision beyond that established framing result.

Completed work is engineering calibration only. An original inert integer-grid
adapter reproduces a restricted, pinned Inspect Robots movement guard. There
were zero mismatches across 11,025 supported native state/target comparisons.
All 224 scripted control traces matched the native interface and passed a
separate event-log replay implementation. These checks do not constitute
independent human review or LLM results. Rotated scenes are dependent and four
duplicate observations must be removed before model collection. AI assistance
was used in engineering and drafting.

Before any query, seek method criticism and freeze nonduplicate cases, sampling,
model versions, endpoints, scoring, exclusion rules, uncertainty analysis,
spending and rejection criteria. Compare naming variants with equivalent
information. Separately test a repair adding explicit guard exclusions, and a
broader-coverage prevention control. Distinguish feasible safe detours from
infeasible tasks, unwanted executed contact from verbal refusal, and prevention
from loss of useful completion. Cluster uncertainty by base scene. Use correct
and deliberately faulty controls, replay every attempted trace independently,
and reserve unopened confirmation cases. Publish nulls, invalid outputs,
technical failures, disagreements, uncertainty and rejected contributions.
