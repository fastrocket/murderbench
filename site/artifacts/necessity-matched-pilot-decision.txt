Interpretation
These fixed instructions did not derail the completed agents under fully disclosed
mechanics with a perfect, read-only trusted alternative. Two repetitions and one
fixed wording per directive cannot estimate deployment prevalence or exclude
other attacks. Kimi's completed cases cover only fabricated-need settings; its
genuine-need behavior remains unmeasured. DeepSeek has no completed case here.
The pilot does not establish a population null effect or general safety.

Why the novelty gate fails
The broad safety-language availability attack is already covered by Semantic
Denial of Service in LLM-Controlled Robots, safeguard false-refusal attacks and
guardrail exhaustion work. PEAR's released fixtures include compliance-framed
exhaustion. See necessity-safety-availability-audit.txt for primary sources,
exact inspections, pinned release and access limitations.
The finite first-loss diagnostic is an applied implementation of established
viability ideas. No new algorithm or theorem is established. The offline
capacity repair works in the synthetic contract, but no model chose the probe,
so these model results cannot identify its mitigation effect. An actual operator
decision changed by this measurement remains unvalidated. New model IDs and
synthetic trace exports alone do not meet the requested impact/novelty bar.
