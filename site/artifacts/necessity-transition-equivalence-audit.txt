TRANSITION-LEVEL EQUIVALENCE AUDIT: TWO CLOSE IMPLEMENTATIONS
3 October 2026 UTC. Partial audit; does not clear the full novelty gate.

Question
Can a released fixture distinguish the paired pending-operation case in which
current verification takes time, cancellation becomes effective after its
acknowledgement, replacement can miss a rescue deadline, and stopping leaves
background commitment active? Compare transitions and endpoints, not labels.

Local reference
benchmark/necessity_pending.py implements submission before tick advancement,
delivery before physical events, configurable cancellation/commit tie order,
irreversible expiry, delayed replacement, and settlement after stopping.
reviews/necessity-timing-boundary.json records48 verified fixed-policy traces.
These are public development controls. They do not establish a new metric,
an all-policy feasibility frontier or independently authored confirmation cases.

Decision
These selected implementations do not invalidate the specific pending-race
fixture, but do invalidate broad novelty claims about time, evidence or harmful
inaction. A mechanism difference alone does not establish a research contribution.
The stronger claim still needs a working option-preservation/repair diagnostic
and comparison against Cordon, Outcome Finality, AgentAbstain and the partial-
observation assurance theory. A programmable framework may support an extension
without having reported the same experiment. Separate representability from
published fixture and measured result. Independent review remains unsecured.

Access and reproducibility
Primary files were fetched by their exact URLs in the existing inventories and
checked with SHA256 before reading selected ranges. A browser request initially
used an incorrect pressure path and returned404; the inventory's correct pinned
URL succeeded. No404 was treated as absence of implementation. A GitHub browser
tree view was restricted; direct pinned public files succeeded. No author was
contacted and no access terms were accepted.

This audit changes no frozen simulator, collector, analysis or manifest. Full
collection remains conditional on remaining contribution, access and budget gates.
