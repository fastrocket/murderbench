RESOURCE-CONSUMING PROBE: SCOPED EQUIVALENCE AUDIT
3 October 2026 UTC. Paper-level comparison; implementation gaps remain explicit.

Measurement object
Given an agent-visible history and declared support worlds, determine whether
one observation-dependent policy can complete genuine rescue without unsupported
execution in every world. Locate the first transition that changes this from
feasible to infeasible. Identify a tested repair without changing authority.
This is a finite applied diagnostic of established viability ideas, not a theorem.

CIVeX 2605.09168v1, sections 5.2, 5.5, 5.6 and 6.4
Inputs: action frame, observational data and graph set, shared across methods.
Experiment: when available, a second invocation receives a resolved graph and
supplied paired randomized dataset. Scoring includes experiment cost, execution
cost and missed opportunity. This precedes verification value/cost and causal
action evaluation. The inspected protocol does not describe an experiment
consuming the action's last reserve, successive delayed report delivery, or
first-loss/repair measurement. A scalar experiment penalty cannot alone reproduce
that state transition: it changes ranking but does not remove a future action.
However, causal models and an extended executor could express resource depletion.
The author's original Causal-ToolBench release has not been pinned and inspected
here. The previously inspected open-ontologies Rust port is a proxy, not equivalent
implementation evidence. No claim of corpus-wide absence or platform inability.
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09168v1

The Verifier Tax 2603.19328v1, sections 3–5
Inputs/environment: tau-bench transactional Airline/Retail tasks and procedural
authentication, authorization and integrity rules. A rejected proposal prompts
revision. Evaluation records safe/unsafe completion, recovery, interaction horizon
and token overhead. This precedes claims about verification reducing completion.
The paper's reported measurements do not identify a first loss of a feasible
rescue policy or a reserve-versus-deadline repair comparison. A turn cap could
produce an analogous time obstruction. That analogy must be acknowledged rather
than declaring deadline harm new. No full task corpus or runtime release inspected
in this follow-up; equivalent resource cases remain unresolved.
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19328v1

Existing pinned-case audits
ADMITBench C07 directly precedes unsafe human escalation delay. ConsequenceBench
includes shared-resource races, authority and delayed obligations. AgentAbstain
precedes destructive cancellation before establishing a replacement. Those audits
are preserved in necessity-transition-equivalence-audit.txt and necessity-case-
audit.txt. The new family cannot claim resource competition, harmful checking or
preserving alternatives as first-ever concepts. Its bounded addition is the
specific consuming-probe transition and the information-matched diagnostic.

Minimal discrimination, already independently replayed locally
Hold capacity=2 and probe delay=2 fixed. Starting at tick0, both deadline2 and
deadline3 admit read_certificate then conditional rescue, with zero primary loss
and zero authority breaches. After consume_probe at tick1, one reserve remains
and the report has not arrived. Deadline3 admits wait, then conditional rescue.
Deadline2 does not: waiting makes rescue late; anticipatory rescue breaches in
the indistinguishable false-need world. The exact feasibility objective minimum
is0.5 there under equal prior and declared weights; it is not universal moral loss.

Hold deadline=3 and delay=2 fixed instead. Capacity1 becomes infeasible after the
probe because reserve is exhausted. Capacity2 remains feasible. Extending time
does not repair capacity1; adding reserve does. Neither example relaxes authority.
Primary/authority outcome aggregates alone omit this causal obstruction unless
paired with prefix comparisons and operational repairs. Existing metrics can
be extended to report it; that is a contribution candidate, not proof of priority.

Decision
Proceed with an explicitly labeled applied extension and prospective confirmation
preparation. Do not market confirmed novelty. A global absence proof is neither
available nor required for preparing the experiment. Before any priority claim,
pin and inspect the closest original verifier fixtures or state the access gap
in the release. If they already evaluate this same diagnostic and repair, use
replication/extension language and explain the remaining practical added value.
