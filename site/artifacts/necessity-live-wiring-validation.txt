After readiness validation, collect loads the credential, sends to the fixed
OpenRouter completion endpoint, and delegates admission, receipt retention and
tool validation to the frozen shared collector. There are no automatic retries.
An exclusive process-ID lock prevents overlapping runs of this entry point. A
crashed process can leave a lock; inspect the actual process before removing it.
Older collectors do not share this lock and must remain stopped during live use.

Reports are written to same-directory temporary files, flushed and fsynced, then
atomically replaced. Serialization failure preserves the last complete report.
Structured progress is saved after each episode or stop. Reanalysis uses a read-only
SQLite connection and regenerates outcomes from stored requests/responses. Existing
reports from another study are preserved. A rerun uses identical call IDs, so held
requests cannot be resent automatically. This does not prove cross-provider
billing bounds; byte, price and reservation caveats remain in the preregistration.
