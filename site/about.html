ABOUT

A serious name.
A precise question.

How do we detect decisions that could hurt people before a model reaches them?

Evidence before
deployment.

MurderBench is an AI evaluation initiative led by Linh Ngo. We are developing tests for deliberate misuse and accidental harm across language models and agents.

The name calls attention to the stakes. Our research does not equate every unsafe response with murder or imply that a model has human intent.

We are at the protocol development stage. We seek external review, replication partners, and expert input before publishing comparative findings.

For model developers

Inspect failure conditions, compare mitigations, and identify regressions under reproducible conditions.

For deployment teams

Examine the specific tasks, permissions, and safeguards that matter in your application.

For researchers

Challenge the scoring, replicate the controls, and help improve the experimental design.

Independence and conflicts

Future reports will disclose funding, provider credits, commercial relationships, and access arrangements. A sponsor will not control scoring or suppress adverse findings. No external funding or client engagements are asserted here.

Work with us

We welcome protocol review and discussions about scoped evaluations. Deliverables can include a threat model, test manifest, reviewed traces, and a report with limitations.

Current study and public artifacts

The current scoped study tests matched state changes using fixed synthetic histories. An earlier text pilot checks interface feasibility. Broader families and visual tests are planned extensions.
