MurderBench is an AI agent evaluation initiative led by Linh Ngo and operated by XP.COM, LLC dba Xenocom.

Why this exists

Agents act through tools and safeguards. We want operators to know whether those safeguards enforce the constraint they care about, and whether a repair preserves the useful task.

We are building a focused engineering evaluation practice. We do not represent ourselves as a university, accredited research lab or a government-supported program.

Who does the work

Linh Ngo leads the initiative. AI coding and research tools assist with implementation, source discovery and analysis. That assistance is disclosed; automated review is not represented as external peer review.

No independent reviewers, lab partnerships or funding awards are secured for the proposed guardrail study. We are seeking them and will disclose material relationships.

What earns publication

A replayable result with clear denominators and uncertainty—or a documented reason to reject an idea. We preserve failed controls, incomplete collections and negative findings.
